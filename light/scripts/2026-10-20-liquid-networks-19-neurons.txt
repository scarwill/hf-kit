A liquid neuron is different. Its state doesn't just jump to an answer. It flows moment by moment like a small equation that keeps running. To be clear, liquid networks freeze their weights after training, too. What stays flexible is how they behave while they're running. That's why they're called liquid. Think of ice and water.
