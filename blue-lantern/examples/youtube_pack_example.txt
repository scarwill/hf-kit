DESCRIPTION
Ten quiet minutes to slow down. No talking: just follow the circle.
It grows as you breathe in and shrinks as you breathe out. Use it before studying, before an exam or a meeting, when you feel anxious, or to wind down before sleep.

How it works
• Relaxed breathing (4 in · 6 out): a longer out-breath helps the body settle.
• Box breathing (4 in · 4 hold · 4 out · 4 hold): steady and even, a well-known focus technique.
• Deep calm (5 in · 7 out): slower still, about 5 breaths a minute.
• At the end, breathe naturally and notice how you feel.

Tips
• Breathe through your nose if you can, gently, never forcing.
• If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop the pattern and breathe normally.
• This is a relaxation exercise, not medical advice or treatment.
