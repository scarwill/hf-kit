DESCRIPTION
The PHP2Shell fix had a secret release date. It leaked three ways: the bug IDs on forums, an exploit video posted by mistake, and Chainguard shipping the fix six hours early. But Amazon says its honeypot was hit on October 17, three days before any leak. No successful hack is confirmed yet. A third PHP bug (CVE-2026-115560) has no patch, but it only works with a rare setup.
Update to PHP 8.5.12 / 8.4.27 / 8.3.36 / 8.2.35 and set session.upload_progress.enabled=0.
