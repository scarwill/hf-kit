A company that tells your browser which websites are real had a secret key.
The key was the number four.
Your browser padlock works because a company like this signs for each site.
Its private key should be a number nobody could ever guess.
India's eMudhra had eight keys all smaller than two hundred and fifty six.
With them anyone could fake a certificate for any website.
Mozilla asked an AI agent to crack them. It did on the first try.
Firefox blocked them within three hours.
eMudhra says no real certificates were made with them. And it is quitting the website certificate business.
But Mozilla says its questions still have no answers.
eMudhra blamed social media. Google called that not acceptable.
If your site uses an emSign certificate start moving off it now.
