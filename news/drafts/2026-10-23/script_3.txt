This terminal is fake. Every letter on it is a guess.
No shell. No files. No computer behind it.
Just a tiny AI model with three hundred and sixty million parameters.
You press a key. It predicts how the screen should change.
It runs vim and tmux. It even plays snake.
It learned from three hundred and thirty six thousand real terminal sessions.
The whole thing is two hundred and eight megabytes and runs in your browser.
But it is a dream of a computer.
Add a line to a file. Read it back. It often forgets.
For developers this is a peek at apps that are learned instead of coded.
So next time your terminal acts weird. Ask yourself. Is it even real?
