DESCRIPTION
WorldTUI is a terminal with nothing behind it: no shell, no files, no computer. A 360M-parameter model (fine-tuned from Qwen2.5-Coder 0.5B) predicts how the screen changes after every keypress. It runs vim, tmux and snake, learned from 336k recorded terminal sessions, and the 208 MB demo runs locally in your browser with WebGPU. The catch: it is a dream of a computer, and it often forgets what you wrote to a file.
We ran the demo ourselves for this video (headless Chrome, software WebGPU, so ours was slow; it is meant for a real GPU).
